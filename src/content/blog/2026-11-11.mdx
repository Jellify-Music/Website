it's Violet, and I wanted to share some awesome updates to Jellify! I've got a variety of fixes and features i'm super jazzed about :3

[tl;dr](#tldr) at the bottom as always.

## [I am a loudspeaker](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fidPIcruCro)

Google Cast support has been fully integrated into Jellify! Y’all can expect a more reliable casting experience with these changes. Jellify now casts your entire queue, not just a singular track, and can be controlled with the volume buttons and player buttons.

## [I woke up in car](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2iCGJoEFxdY)

Android Auto is now fully in drive! You'll see a home screen with your recently and most played tracks, tabs for your artists, albums, and playlists, and have the ability to see the currently playing queue.

1.4 brings this same setup to CarPlay - and it’s available via TestFlight or by sideloading the release.

## [I’m fixin’ to thrill](https://youtu.be/pTR0lMxri5w?is=1oZ7Lwr3shOapgdC)

There’s been a laundry list of fixes made - 1.3 onward should feel more stable, and have addressed a Jellyfin 12 compatibility issue we had around playlists. We also added some optimization to shuffling and made it so “Play Next” on a track that’s already in the queue just moves it up next in the queue. Playback controls now work properly when playing transcoded tracks, and a new first-time contributor added direct AAC playback to Android.

## [I'm gonna keep on growing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h0kXfPrHHcw)

As we round out 2026, we plan on improving our network detection and offline mode. This work is already underway and makes the on-the-go experience a lot nicer. Jellify will detect if your device can connect to the Jellyfin server, and let you know if it's unable to reach it. If the server is unreachable, Jellify will drop into offline mode, applying filters to show only the content that has been cached locally.

This will also come with the ability to control playback remotely, allowing you to control playback on Jellify from another device, as well as UI enhancements to the storage manager for better visibility and management of cached content.

## [Epilogue: I’m alive and amplified](https://youtu.be/vTABPqiYJC4?is=i4xbDeM1pEcfuFvn)

Jellify has been my passion project now for the last 2 years, and it has been the journey of a lifetime.
